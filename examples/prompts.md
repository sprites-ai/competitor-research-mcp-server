# Example prompts for the Competitor Research MCP server

These work as written in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex, Gemini CLI and the other clients listed in the README. Anything that changes the account comes back as an approval first.

## Read and report

- With Sprites, show me every ad Gymshark is running on Meta right now and which have run longest.
- Ask Sprites what Google search ads HubSpot is running for CRM terms.
- Use Sprites to compare our organic keywords with notion.so and list the gaps worth writing for.
- Have Sprites check who ChatGPT recommends for "best project management tool for agencies" and which pages it cites.
- Ask Sprites which backlinks our competitor has that we do not.

## Change, create and launch

- With Sprites, write five Meta ad concepts based on the hooks our top three competitors use.

## Recurring

- Use Sprites to send me a Monday digest of new ads from our five main competitors.

## Tips

- Name the campaign, ad set or page in full. Sprites receives only the request, not the earlier chat.
- Say the period ("last 30 days", "this quarter"); your client passes today's date so it resolves correctly.
- Working on several businesses? Say "switch to <business name>" first (`choose_business`).
- To compare channels, ask about them in one request, for example "compare Google and Meta CPA last month".
